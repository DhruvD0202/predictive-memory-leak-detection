# Reference audit and relevance map

External sources checked: the original 36 works on 14 September 2026; ten additional IEEE/ACM works on 15 September 2026. Current scope: the complete manuscript, including the expanded Literature Survey, Abstract/Keywords and Conclusion. References have been renumbered in order of first citation. The earlier bibliographic entries are preserved apart from their numbers; the project experiments and findings are unchanged. See `DRAFTING_NOTES.md`, `RESULTS_EVIDENCE.md`, `REFERENCE_ADDITIONS.md` and `FINAL_DRAFT_CHECKS.md` for evidence maps and checks. Project results are not attributed to external papers, and cited methods are not described as implemented unless they are present in the code.

The completed draft contains **46 distinct works**, each cited in the manuscript. The ten additions comprise six IEEE-published works, including one IEEE/ACM ICSE paper, and four ACM-published works. The overall bibliography remains a mixture of journal/conference publications, books, a dataset and explicitly identified arXiv records; it is not described as 46 peer-reviewed research articles. Different versions or hosting pages of one work count once.

Google Scholar is a discovery index rather than a publisher or a guarantee of peer review. Its search page was not accessible during this update. IEEE/ACM bibliographic identity and relevance were checked using accessible publisher records, publisher-deposited Crossref metadata and original papers or institutional records. Some direct publisher pages were also unavailable, so the reading scope for each addition is explicit below. Citation counts and impact factors were not used or invented.

Verification means bibliographic identity and the limited claims used in the draft were checked against publisher pages, original paper text, author/institutional records or publisher-deposited Crossref metadata. It does **not** mean every cited work was read in full. Subscription abstracts support only the broad descriptions stated in the survey, not unexamined implementation details or numerical comparisons. No results from another paper are presented as results of this project.

The original Review 1 report was read in full. Source titles, authors and dates were corrected where necessary; its references were not copied without checking. Titles use the published wording; ASCII transliterations of diacritics are used in the current PDF bibliography where needed.

## Scope of the survey

This is a focused narrative survey, not a systematic review or a claim of exhaustive coverage. The Research Gap and Problem Statement reuse the verified sources to motivate a bounded empirical evaluation question; they do not assert that prior work has never combined forecasting and leak detection. Proposed Methodology reuses existing citations and was checked against the implementation and protocols, as recorded in DRAFTING_NOTES.md. No new experimental results were generated during drafting. The completed results, Abstract and Conclusion preserve the verified Colab outcomes; Section 8 and Table 9 separately identify unperformed work. Future revisions must retain those evidence boundaries.
